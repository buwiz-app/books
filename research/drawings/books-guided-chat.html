Templates: start a job with one tap

Pick a template. The assistant asks one thing at a time, with tap-able answers, and ends with a card you confirm. Grey lines name the tool it used; Books tool names are proposals.

1st of each monthMonth-end nudgeChecks what is left before closing last month and leaves a quiet card. Changes nothing.
Routine 'Month-end nudge' reads Books only.
Daily · with the BIR appBIR pair checkReads the BIR draft (tax option, what was handed over) and flags hand-offs that went out of date.
Routine 'BIR pair check' reads the BIR app's read-only tools, approved once. Changes nothing.

Rox allows 8 routines per app. A routine can change Books only through tools it names at install, and you see that list before you approve.

What the assistant can and cannot do with Books

Can

  • Read anything in Books to answer you
  • Draft an entry from a receipt or a bank line
  • Suggest categories and matches
  • Show amounts on cards you asked for
  • Read the BIR draft's tax option

Cannot, without your click

  • Post, void, or change a posted entry
  • Close or reopen a month
  • Hand totals to the BIR app
  • Set your 8% or graduated choice (that lives in BIR)
  • See any TIN (Books does not store them)
