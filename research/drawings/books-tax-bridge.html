How do we map someone else's chart?

Map 1: match each account once new proposal

When you import, Books shows every account with a suggested kind. You confirm or change it. Try the dropdowns.

The suggestion uses the account name, its type in the old system, and what its lines look like. Nothing counts toward a BIR box until you confirm. "Not sure yet" holds the boxes it could touch.

All the other questions

Questions people ask

Do I have to switch to the Books chart of accounts?

No. Keep yours. Match each account to a kind once. New accounts ask for a kind when you create them.

Who does the matching, me or the AI?

The AI suggests; you confirm. Your accountant can review the list. Nothing counts toward a BIR box until it is confirmed.

Does the account name or number decide the box?

Never. Only line facts and Books totals do. That is why any chart works.

Can one account feed two boxes?

Yes, through totals. Sales feed gross sales (1701-MS IV.5) and net sales (2551Q row 1). A single line never counts twice in the same box.

What if one account mixes two kinds?

Mark it mixed. Each line then gets its own kind. The AI proposes per line; you confirm.

What if I change a match later?

Books lists every hand-off that used those lines and marks it out of date. Refresh, check, hand over again.

Where do the box rules come from?

They ship with Books: one rule set per form edition, checked against the BIR app's own form, with open items sent for tax review.

What happens when BIR changes a form?

A new edition gets a new rule set. Old hand-offs keep the version they used, so they can be rebuilt exactly.

Does Books compute any tax?

No. Books adds up your entries. The BIR app applies rates, computes tax due, penalties and due dates.

Who picks 8% or graduated?

You, in the BIR app, at the start of the year. Books reads it and fills the matching boxes.

Why is a box "held"?

Its rule needs a tax review or a fact Books does not have. Books shows why and sends nothing for it.

Why does a return need my OK?

Returns change sales for a period. Books asks once so the total is right, then remembers.

How do 2307s count?

Only when received, and by the quarter printed on the certificate, not the day you entered it.

Cash or accrual?

Totals follow your book's basis, cash by default. Every hand-off states the basis; BIR decides if it fits the form.

I start using Books in October. What about January to September?

Import bank statements for those months, or enter opening totals with proof. They show as opening figures in every box that uses them.

I already filed earlier quarters. What if Books disagrees?

Books lists the difference and the lines behind it. Whether to amend is your call, in the BIR app.

I have two businesses.

Each book links to its own tax profile. Maps and totals never mix.

What about branches?

One tax profile per book in the first version. Branches come later.

I file a joint 1701-MS with my spouse.

Spouse income is refused in the first version. Your own boxes still work.

I'm VAT-registered.

The 2550Q bridge comes later. Today, VAT collected is always kept out of sales.

My payroll comes from another provider.

Import the pay register. Each column is matched to a pay kind once, like accounts.

Does my TIN go anywhere?

No. Books stores no TIN. Values go to the BIR app, which already has it.

Can the AI hand over to BIR by itself?

No. It can prepare. Handing over is your click.

Can I see why a box has its number?

Click it. You get every line behind it, with dates and amounts.

What if a total is negative?

It is not proposed. Books shows it with its lines so you can see why.

Can my accountant use their own chart?

Yes. Their accounts are matched once, like any import. The boxes come out the same.
